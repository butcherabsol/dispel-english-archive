Game Feature

The high resolution, stunning graphics and 3D effects you have ever seen before, will take you real magic world.

  • In Dispel, you will experience all the graphic effect that 3D game has, such as Lights, Glass, and Reflection. Plus all 3D rendered characters look like alive.

Map System: Four big sections on unforgettable adventure.

  • There are four big areas and each area features individual trait. For example, first area called 'Daemonlon' Section contains dark and poisonous swamps take about 20 hours playtime.

Character Create System: Make your own character.

  • What you want is what you get. You can choose every specific appearance by selecting gender, outlook, accessories, and so on. As a matter of course, you can choose your job, such as Warrior, Mage, Thief and so on. During playing game, you may change your outlook by wearing new amours. What You See Is What You Wear.

Over 40 magic spells throughout Good and Evil.

  • In Dispel world, there are good and evil exist together. And you will get all the spells no matter what it is good and evil.

Unique artificial intellectual units

  • They see, think, and act like human players. No more dumb robots in Dispel.

Server Based Network Game System.

  • With Maximum 16 Player Characters, you may take adventure on network. By Dispel's superior Character Create System, you will not see they look same. Either make party or fight against them, It is all up to you.

Realistic Time and Weather.

  • At the evening, the sunsets, then scary night comes. In the morning the sunrises with another spiritual day begins. Sometimes it rains, sometimes it snows. Just like real world, Time and Weather go by.

Real Time Free Battle System.

  • Your will encounter AI based monsters while your journey go on through 5 continents and over 30 cities, town, and dungeon. They don't just wait to fight on specific areas but act and hang around all the places by their own wills. Remember, they are not just stupid objects just like in other RPG. About 100 Monsters live, think, and try to kill you.
