Game Story

There were four kingdoms (DEAMONLON, AESH, YAMM, SHEREG) in the DMAN. The papal court was located in the middle of AESH where is one of the most peaceful kingdom. It had been covering the entire religious sacrificial rate for reciprocal four neighboring kingdoms.

The history of DMAN had been built up markedly with their religious thought.It seemed as if the pope confined his effort to building stable peaces for neighboring kingdoms.After several years later, out of the blue, he turned out paganism and many religion group tried to protest against heretical expansion. However, it was useless efforts.
Eventually, the pope declared a war against his opposite side to conquer entire territories.As a result, many people died owing to different doctrine and territories had been desolated. It caused rest of all the opposite sides to become the resistance.

The pioneer of resistance, SHEM, was a passionate youth who got a strong charisma and pure spirit. Surprisingly, he had a special ability, which he could handle Dragon uniquely. He had desperately been fighting against the Pope＊s force with his unique ability.

The young ADON had been grown in countryside in DEAMONLON. He was so frustrated in what he had been living in countryside. So, he took a journey to NAHASHI which is capital of DAEMONON. During his journey, he knew background of war and despotism of the pope.

Now the story will be led by your unimaginable situation
